# Factory Intelligence

### An Intelligence Layer for SME Manufacturing

Factory Intelligence is a prototype solution developed for the **Schneider Electric Hackathon**.

It is designed around a simple idea:

> **SME factories do not only need more data. They need a system that turns factory data into low-risk, measurable decisions — and learns from the outcomes.**

The prototype demonstrates how an existing factory can move from:

**Observe → Understand → Simulate → Decide → Implement → Verify → Remember → Improve**

---

## 1. The Problem

Indian SME factories often operate with:

- Ageing machinery
- Limited real-time energy visibility
- Manual operating practices
- Fragmented production, energy and maintenance data
- Limited engineering resources
- High sensitivity to downtime and CAPEX
- Difficulty proving whether an energy-efficiency intervention actually delivered savings

The challenge is therefore not simply detecting energy waste.

The harder problem is:

> **How do we convert an identified efficiency opportunity into a safe, economically justified, verified and repeatable factory improvement?**
